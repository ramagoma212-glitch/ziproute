Delivery tips & courier know-how

Welcome to the ZipRoute Blog. This page shares simple advice about preparing parcels, using motorcycle courier services and making delivery information clear and accurate.

01Parcel Prep

5 ways to prepare a parcel for delivery

Use strong packaging, protect fragile items, seal the parcel securely, write the correct recipient details and check the size and weight before arranging collection.

02Courier Tips

Why motorcycle couriers can be practical

For suitable small deliveries, motorcycles can be useful in busy urban areas because of their compact size. Delivery time still depends on traffic, weather, distance and road conditions.

03Safety

Make delivery information clear

Provide a complete address, a working phone number and the recipient’s name. Clear information reduces avoidable delays and makes handover easier.

Quick delivery checklist

Secure packaging • Correct address • Working phone number • Suitable parcel size • Confirmed collection and delivery arrangements.
